Enter visible motif/text and choose “Check public reference”. Europe uses the deep period/series structure. North America, Asia and Oceania use the priority model: older, precious-metal, transition and selected commemorative coins receive deeper coverage, while ordinary modern circulation is kept as a compact baseline. Unresolved physical details are explicitly marked for verification rather than guessed. Priority uses three levels: Interesting, Possibly interesting and Normal.

What this Beta does—and does not do

It creates a private, photo-linked coin inventory and can experimentally detect/crop coin-shaped objects. Public-reference matching is a research aid, not a guarantee of identification, rarity, value, authenticity, composition or grade.
