A practical first step for coin collectors

The goal is not to replace specialist research. It is to give you a structured starting point so you know what you have, what needs checking and what you want to investigate further.

📷

Start with photos

Photograph your coins in manageable groups. When using front and reverse photos, keep each coin in the same position.

🪙

Build an inventory

Create structured coin records you can review and improve instead of relying on an unorganized camera roll.

🔎

Focus your research

Mark records that deserve a closer look and continue research with suitable public reference sources.

What the Browser Beta is designed to do

Help create a structured coin inventory
Support front/reverse photo matching
Allow records to be reviewed and edited
Highlight coins for further research
Support public-reference follow-up
Export inventory data to Excel or CSV
Important: the Beta does not guarantee coin identification, authenticity, rarity or value. It is an inventory and research-support tool.

A simple collecting workflow

Photograph

Take clear front photos. For multiple coins, keep their positions fixed before photographing the reverse.

Organize

Create and review structured records, adding notes where useful.

Research & export

Investigate priority records further and save the inventory in Excel or CSV.
